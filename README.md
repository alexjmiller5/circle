## Infrastructure

- Monitor the logs for the macOS process bird to see the last iCloud sync time for monitoring syncing of calls
- Uses a SQLite database to access call history
- Will be written all in Swift
- Authentication and sync through iCloud

## Usability Issues

- Potential issues with low power mode due to iCloud sync being off with that
- When you call someone from the app tell them we’ll sync the call later

## Feature Ideas

- set good calling hours for each person -- won't suggest people to call outside of these hours
- color code people based on priority of when to be calling them
- meticuluosly create the default settings to be optimal for ease of use for users
- fun notifications to remind people to call their people with emojis and bringing comedy and joy into them. These kinds of suggestions are key -- they require good customizability and care
- last call and last messaged feature
- facetime / phone call support
- imessage support
- whatsapp calling and message support
- Voice memo support for leaving messages for people
- Have a list of people to call that is sorted by priority, their name and contact with the last time you called them listed under it, with an icon for imessage, phone call, facetime, whatsapp call and whatsapp message
- store the user call logs temporarily in the cloud for fastest sync, but give the users the option to never store it in the cloud and only send it directly to iPhone from the mac
- Can set goals for how much you want to stay in touch with people and track how much you stick to those goals for app idea
- Sharing pictures with people to catch up on which should have a toggle bar for different people and maybe have a filter for who to send pictures of who to. Basically you should send pictures of yourself to mom cause you haven’t seen her in a while or you should send pictures of the boys you chilled with this weekend. Or if you have a certain hobby in common with other people, you could share pictures related to that hobby that AI analyzes
- Allow people to select their preferences for contacting others in general as well as for each individual person, such as preferring to call or text, and then the app can suggest the best way to contact them based on how they usually contact them
- app can suggest people who might be in your inner circle based on how often you contact them and how long you talk to them
- App can try to learn how you are when you catch up with people -- are you more interested in logging any contact at all, or do you differentiate between just sending someone an instagram reel and having a real conversation with them? It can then suggest people to call based on that
- Add suggested and modifiable templates for how to start a conversation with people, such as "Hey, I was thinking about you the other day and wanted to see how you're doing" or "I saw this article and thought of you, what do you think?" This could help users overcome the initial hurdle of starting a conversation.
